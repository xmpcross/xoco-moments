## Morning, above the water

The chapel holds forty people and the doors stay open, so the ceremony ran to the sound of wind in the pines. Nobody moved to close them.

## The long walk down

Between the vows and the table there is always an hour that belongs to no one. We spent it on the cliff path, walking slowly, mostly not photographing.

## Two drives up the same road

The ridge is photographed a thousand times a year and almost never at both ends of the day. Everything here came from one road, fourteen hours apart.

Dinner ran past midnight under a single strand of bulbs. The last frame of the night is Marc carrying his grandmother’s chair back up the hill.
