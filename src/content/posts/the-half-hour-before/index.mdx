## Stay by the window

Find the light in the room, stand near it, and wait. Everything eventually comes to the window.

## Nobody performing

Makeup half done, a shoe strap that would not sit, the room getting quieter as the hour came. None of this was asked for, which is why it works.

## Staying out of the way

Nothing in this half hour is asked for, which means standing still, working quietly, and letting the room forget you are in it. The moment you direct it, it stops being that half hour.

No posed shoes. No ring on a book.
