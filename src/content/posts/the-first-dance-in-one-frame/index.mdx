Flash would have made a clean picture of a room nobody was in. The blur is the room.

## The rest of the night

## After the frame

The rest of the night ran between a thirtieth and a fifteenth of a second, braced against whatever was nearest. Half of it is unusable and the other half is this gallery.

One frame from about four hundred. The rest are in here too.
