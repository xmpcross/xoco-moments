## The vows

They wrote their own, then swapped them the night before so each would read the other's. Neither made it to the end without stopping.

<Quote attribution="Simone">
  Pastor Ruth taught us both to read. She was not going to let anyone else do this.
</Quote>

## Why the room worked

White walls hand back whatever comes through the window, which is how forty people in a small room photographed cleanly with nothing added. The arch came down the same afternoon.

The whole thing took twenty-five minutes. The lunch afterwards took five hours.
