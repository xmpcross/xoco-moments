## Before anyone else was awake

The cold does something to the colour that no filter reproduces: everything goes blue until the sun clears the ridge, and then for about four minutes it is gold.

## What it costs to get there

Eleven kilometres, two hours in the dark, and a registrar who walks faster than either of them. Everything in the bag has to earn its weight at that hour.

We were back at the refuge by one, eating soup, boots steaming by the fire. There is no reception in this gallery because there wasn’t one.
