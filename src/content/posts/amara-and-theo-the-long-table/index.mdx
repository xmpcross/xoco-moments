## Setting up

Two hours before anyone arrived, the light came sideways through the cypress and the table was still empty. That is usually my favourite picture of the day.

## One table, all evening

Nobody changed seats, which almost never happens, and the whole dinner stayed inside one frame because of it. The lemons went back against the wall the next morning.

The band packed up at two. The table was still full at three.
