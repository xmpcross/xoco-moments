## Fewer, better decisions

A digital body will make four hundred frames of a first dance. A film body makes eleven, and one of them is the picture.

<Quote attribution="Marta">You took half as many and we kept twice as many.</Quote>

## How it works here

A third of every wedding goes on film, through one lab, then gets edited beside the digital frames as a single set. Nobody is ever told which is which.

Everything is shot digitally as well. Nobody has ever needed the backup, and it goes in the bag anyway.
